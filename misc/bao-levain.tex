\dish{老面包子}
\hiragana{lǎomiàn bāozi}
\altdish{Bao, levain}
\altdish{Steamed buns, levain}
%\serves{}
\makes{14}
\prep{4--5 hours}
\source{modernist.bread}

\begin{ingredients}
  \ingr{230}{g}{water}
  \ingr{45}{g}{nonfat milk powder}
  \ingr{2.5}{g}{dry yeast}
  \ingrS{150}{g}{levain, young (100\% hydration)}
  \ingr{395}{g}{bread flour}
  \ingr{85}{g}{sugar (55~g for apple fillings)}
  \ingr{55}{g}{potato starch}
  \ingr{20}{g}{vegetable oil}
  \ingr{10}{g}{salt}
\end{ingredients}


\begin{recipe}
  \begin{enumerate}

  \item Combine water, milk powder and yeast.  Stir to dissolve.  Add
    levain.

  \item Add remaining ingredients and mix on low speed to a shaggy
    mass.  Mix on medium speed to full gluten development.

  \item Transfer to an oiled tub and cover.  Bulk ferment
    90--150~minutes at 25--26\degreeC, until noticeably risen (not
    necessarily doubled).

  \item Divide into 14 balls of 70~g each.  Preform as boules.  Rest
    10--15~minutes.

  \item Prepare 14~squares of parchment paper and brush lightly with
    oil.

  \item Shape a round bun, then flatten to a disc, rolling the edges
    thinner than the middle.  Place 30~g of filling at the center,
    bring the edges to a close around the filling.  Place each bun on
    an oiled piece of parchment paper.

  \item Final proof, 40--70~minutes, until puffy and slow to spring
    back.

  \item Steam, seam-side down, on the parchment paper.

  \item Consume within 10~minutes while the buns are still hot.

  \end{enumerate}
\end{recipe}

\notes
Use levain at or just before its peak, not old and sour.  Timings are
starting estimates; they depend heavily on levain activity and dough
temperature.

Pure levain: 200~g levain, 370~g flour, 205~g water, no yeast.  Bulk
4--6~hours at 26\degreeC, then a long final proof.

\stage{Fillings}

All fillings must be cooled completely and have no free liquid.

\textbf{Black Sesame} (黑芝麻包, \textit{hēi zhīma bāo}).  200~g
black sesame paste, 100~g butter, 100~g powdered sugar, pinch of
salt.

\textbf{Tuna} (金枪鱼包, \textit{jīnqiāngyú bāo}).  200 g tuna
\textit{(if in oil or water: well drained; if fresh, finely chopped
  and briefly cooked)}, 2 scallions,
1\fracH\tsp{} soy sauce, \fracH\tsp{} sugar, white pepper, 1~\tsp{}
sesame oil, 1~\tsp{} neutral oil, \fracH\tsp{} grated ginger. \\
Press and dry tuna.  Mix all.  Use 1--1\fracH\tbsp{} per bao.

\textbf{Egg} (葱花鸡蛋包, \textit{cōnghuā jīdàn bāo}).  4 large eggs,
4 scallions, 1\fracH\tsp{} soy sauce,
\fracQ\tsp{} salt, white pepper, 1~\tsp{} sesame oil (and neutral oil
for cooking).  \\
Beat ingredients until homogeneous.  Soft-scramble, stop early.  Add
scallions and cool.  Use 1--1\fracH\tbsp{} per bao.

\textbf{Apple, ginger, sesame.}  600~g apples (peeled, cored,
5--8~mm dice), 20~g butter, 10--15~g grated ginger, 15~g lemon juice,
1~g salt, 0--20~g sugar, 8~g potato starch, 30~g toasted sesame seeds;
optionally 0.2--0.4~g ground red Sichuan pepper. \\
Sauté apples in butter, uncovered, in a wide pan over medium-high heat
until most moisture has evaporated.  Add ginger, lemon juice, salt,
sugar and Sichuan pepper; cook until dry.  Stir in starch slurried in a
little cold water, cook briefly.  Off heat, fold in sesame.  Makes
about 420~g; apple pieces should stay distinct.

\textbf{Apple, black sesame.}  400~g apples (peeled, cored, small
dice), 20~g butter, 120~g black sesame paste, 15--25~g powdered sugar,
1~g salt. \\
Cook apples in butter until reduced to about 250~g, keeping the
pieces.  Cool, mix with remaining ingredients.  If loose, add ground
toasted sesame.

\textbf{Apple, scallion, miso.}  500~g apples (peeled, cored, diced),
15~g butter or neutral oil, 10~g grated ginger, 50~g scallions (whites
and greens separated), 25~g white miso, 10~g rice vinegar, 5~g sesame
oil; optionally finely chopped shiitake, cooked dry separately. \\
Cook apples in butter until dry but holding shape.  Add ginger and
scallion whites at the end.  Off heat, mix in miso, vinegar, sesame
oil, scallion greens and shiitake.
