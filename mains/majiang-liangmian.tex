\dish{麻酱凉面}
\hiragana{má jiàng liáng miàn}
\altdish{Cold sesame noodles}
\serves{2}
\prep{20 minutes (sauce and noodles can be made hours ahead)}
\source{openai-chatgpt}
\label{majiang-liangmian}

\begin{ingredients}
  \ingr{45}{g}{Chinese sesame paste, or tahini}
  \ingr{1\fracH}{\tbsp}{light soy sauce}
  \ingr{1}{\tbsp}{Chinkiang black vinegar}
  \ingr{1}{tsp}{sesame oil}
  \ingr{1--2}{tsp}{\hyperref[laziyou]{辣椒油} (chili oil), to taste}
  \ingr{1}{tsp}{sugar}
  \ingr{1}{clove}{garlic, finely grated}
  \ingr{\fracH}{tsp}{ginger, finely grated}
  \ingrS{3--5}{\tbsp}{cold water}
  \ingr{200--220}{g}{dried wheat noodles (Chinese wheat noodles, or ramen)}
  \ingr{\fracH}{}{cucumber, julienned}
  \ingr{1--2}{}{scallions, finely sliced (only 1 if large bulb)}
  \ingr{}{}{toasted sesame seeds}
  \ingr{}{}{roasted peanuts, crushed (optional)}
  \ingr{}{}{coriander (optional)}
\end{ingredients}


\begin{recipe}
  \begin{enumerate}

  \item Mix sesame paste, soy sauce, vinegar, sesame oil, chili oil,
    sugar, garlic and ginger. Add water gradually until creamy and
    pourable.

  \item Cook noodles until just done. Drain, rinse under cold water,
    drain very well.

  \item Toss noodles with sauce, cucumber and garnishes.

  \end{enumerate}
\end{recipe}

\notes{} Chinese sesame paste gives a deeper toasted flavour than
tahini; with tahini, add a little more sesame oil or 1~tsp toasted
sesame seeds.

For make-ahead: refrigerate sauce (it thickens; loosen with a splash
of water before serving) or leave at cool room temperature, which
keeps it easier to mix in. Cut cucumber and spring onion ahead and
refrigerate. Noodles can be cooked ahead: after draining, toss with
1~tsp neutral oil, cover, refrigerate --- don't sauce them until
serving, or they turn drier and softer. At serving: loosen sauce,
toss noodles with sauce, add cucumber and garnishes.

Serve alongside \hyperref[jiangcu-fanqie]{姜醋番茄}.
